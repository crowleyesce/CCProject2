This project was inspired by mixing the idea of a deck of cards with the art of the stickers on my water bottle. This eventually led to me thinking about Pokemon cards, so that’s where the drawings of the animals came from. The random tinted overlay also came from the thought of Pokemon, as in the games Pokemon can sometimes appear with rare alternate colors. My initial idea was to recreate the rarity, but I found it to be more within my skill level to simply make a purely random tint.
Another scrapped idea was to have separate packs with 4 types of animals each that could be selected (or randomly chosen for an extra random factor) and opened, but that also ended up being outside of my ability, sadly. 
Since I couldn’t use the random pack as one of my 3 random factors, I thought to label each ‘card’, but I also couldn’t get differing text to line up with specific cards, so I made the text purely random and give a ‘personality’ to the animal you drew.
I wish I could’ve made an artwork closer to my original vision, but I think what I ended up with is cute enough.. Just very simple (SIGH!) I really want to be done with l5 lua, I still can’t get over this weird mental block I have with working in it. I prefer more hands-on porgrams I think.
